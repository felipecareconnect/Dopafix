Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

This privacy policy describes how DopaFix collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and digital products. By using our site, you agree to the practices described here.

What we collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Contact form submissions: Name, email address, and message content when you reach out through our contact form.
  • Assessment data: Your answers to the self-reflection assessment are stored temporarily in your browser's local storage so you can view your results. This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage data: Basic analytics such as page views and referral sources, collected through privacy-focused analytics tools. This data is aggregated and anonymized.

What we do not collect

We do not collect medical information, diagnoses, or sensitive health data. Our assessment is a self-reflection tool, and your answers are not transmitted to our servers.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our website and products
  • Understand how visitors use our site in aggregate

Data storage and security

Contact form submissions are processed through a secure email service. We do not store your messages on our own servers. Assessment results are stored only in your browser's local storage and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no internet transmission is completely secure.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential site functionality and basic analytics. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking across other websites.

Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party services for hosting, analytics, and email delivery. These services have their own privacy policies and are bound by data protection regulations.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Clear your assessment data from your browser at any time

Children's privacy

Our website and products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us.
