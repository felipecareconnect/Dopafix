Refund Policy

We want you to find our tools genuinely useful.

Last updated: August 2026

Our commitment

We create tools to help reduce friction in everyday life. If a product isn't helpful for you, we don't want your money. That's our simple standard.

Eligibility

You may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if:

  • The product does not meet your needs
  • You experienced a technical issue that prevented you from accessing or using the product
  • You accidentally purchased the same product twice

What is not refundable

We cannot issue refunds in the following cases:

  • More than 14 days have passed since purchase
  • You have already downloaded and used the product extensively
  • The refund request is made on behalf of someone else without their authorization

How to request a refund

To request a refund, contact us through our contact form with the following information:

  • The email address used for purchase
  • The name of the product
  • The approximate date of purchase
  • A brief explanation of why the product didn't work for you (this helps us improve)

We aim to process refund requests within 3–5 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Digital product nature

Our products are digital downloads (PDFs). Once a file has been downloaded, it cannot be "returned" in the traditional sense. Our 14-day policy exists because we believe in the value of our tools and want you to feel confident trying them.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or a specific refund request, please contact us and we'll do our best to help.
